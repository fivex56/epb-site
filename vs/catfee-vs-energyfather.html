What the archive has on CatFee and EnergyFather

Across 45 scans with a price from both at 65k energy for one hour, CatFee was in front 27 times and EnergyFather 18 times, with 17 changes of lead in between.

The average distance between them while both were quoting was 1.09 TRX. The widest it opened was 1.49 TRX, at 2026-09-06 11:45 UTC. The movement was one sided: CatFee moved 18 times, between 1.36 and 3.60 TRX and EnergyFather held 2.86 TRX at every one of the 403 scans.

None of that is a trend. The board has been recording since 2026-09-05 07:00 UTC, every 15 minutes, and the archive behind this page is 190 hours of data so far. It is long enough to say what CatFee and EnergyFather did yesterday and too short to say what either of them usually does.

What separates CatFee from EnergyFather

The two do not sell the same ladder. CatFee publishes no price of its own that we can read; EnergyFather publishes one hour prices. In practice EnergyFather quotes one hour where CatFee does not, so 0 of the ten rows above are inferred for CatFee and 7 for EnergyFather.

Neither CatFee nor EnergyFather will rent for 15 minutes. An hour is the shortest thing either of them sells, and the 15 minute row is empty on both sides of the table above for that reason.

Neither CatFee nor EnergyFather publishes an order size limit that our scraper can read, so the order limits table that appears on some head to head pages is absent from this one.

One of these two pays us and the other does not: our EnergyFather link is a referral link, our CatFee link is a plain one. The prices are read the same way for both, and CatFee outranking EnergyFather on this page would cost us money without changing a line of it.

CatFee carries automatic top-ups and a dApp and EnergyFather does not. Both of them run a public API.
